# Interview Packet Template

Use these slots for a full packet; shorten for urgency or sparse evidence. Label unknowns and distinguish facts from inferences within the relevant section.

## Thesis

Connect the role's stated needs to supported experience. Include motivation only when supplied; otherwise leave it as a question to resolve.

## Role map

| Role need | Candidate evidence | Gap or question |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Stories

For each distinct supported example: situation, responsibility, personal action, result and evidence, lesson if known, and relevance to this role. Name missing evidence without filling it in.

## Questions to ask

Select the few unanswered questions that clarify mandate, success measures, team, constraints, or hiring process.

## Practice prompts

Pair plausible round-specific questions with answer outlines and proof points. Identify one or two gaps or sensitive topics to rehearse honestly.

## Logistics

Record known date, timezone, format, and participants. Include preparation materials and a short checklist; mark missing logistics as unknown.

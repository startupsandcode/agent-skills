# Product Brief Contract

## Problem

State the evidence-backed problem and why it matters now.

## Intended user

Name the primary user and the context in which they experience the problem.

## Desired outcome

Describe the user or business outcome, distinct from a proposed implementation.

## Scope

List the behavior included in this one coherent PR and its boundary.

## Non-goals

List explicitly excluded behavior, adjacent work, and later phases.

## Acceptance criteria

List observable, testable outcomes. Each criterion must say what can be checked, by whom or how, and what successful behavior is.

## Constraints

Record applicable technical, delivery, policy, compatibility, or operational constraints.

## Assumptions

Surface assumptions and identify which are consequential to scope, behavior, cost, risk, or feasibility.

## Risks

Identify meaningful delivery, user, operational, security, privacy, or dependency risks and their relevant mitigations.

## Approval-ready check

The brief is approval-ready only when every acceptance criterion is observable, consequential assumptions are surfaced, and the scope fits one coherent PR. If a product choice that could change scope or behavior remains unresolved, report `Needs input` rather than inventing a requirement. Otherwise present this brief with the final implementation plan and wait for direct approval.
